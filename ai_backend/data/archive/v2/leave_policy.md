# Annual Leave Policy

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2025  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Department:** HR  
**Document Type:** Policy  
**Changes:** Increased annual leave from 15 to 20 days

## Overview

This document outlines the company's updated annual leave policy for all full-time employees, effective January 15, 2025.

## Annual Leave Entitlement

All full-time employees are entitled to **20 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.

### What Changed
- **Previous:** 15 days annual leave
- **New:** 20 days annual leave
- **Effective:** January 15, 2025

### Eligibility
- Employees become eligible for annual leave after completing 3 months of continuous employment
- Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month
- Unused leave can be carried forward up to 7 days to the next year (increased from 5 days)

## Leave Application Process

1. Submit leave request at least 2 weeks in advance
2. Manager approval required
3. HR must be notified via the employee portal
4. Emergency leave requires same-day notification

## Public Holidays

In addition to annual leave, employees receive:
- 10 public holidays per year
- Public holidays falling on weekends are now compensated with a floating day off (NEW)

## Special Circumstances

- Medical leave is separate from annual leave (15 days per year)
- Maternity/Paternity leave as per local labor laws
- Compassionate leave available on case-by-case basis
- **NEW:** Bereavement leave - 3 days paid leave for immediate family

## Year-End Carryover

- Unused leave up to 7 days can be carried to next year
- Leave beyond 7 days will be forfeited
- **NEW:** Option to cash out up to 5 unused days at year-end

## Contact

For questions regarding this policy, contact HR at hr@company.com
