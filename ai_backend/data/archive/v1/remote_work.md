# Remote Work Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Department:** HR  
**Document Type:** Policy

## Overview

This policy establishes guidelines for remote work arrangements within the company.

## Eligibility

Remote work is available to employees who:
- Have completed 6 months of employment
- Have received manager approval
- Have suitable home office setup
- Maintain consistent performance standards

## Remote Work Schedule

- Employees may work remotely **1 day per week**
- Remote workdays must be approved in advance
- Core hours (10 AM - 4 PM) must be maintained
- Team meetings require in-office presence unless otherwise specified

## Equipment and Expenses

- Company provides laptop and necessary software
- Employees responsible for internet connectivity
- Office supplies available for pickup from office
- No reimbursement for home office furniture

## Communication Requirements

- Must be available on company chat during work hours
- Video conferencing required for all team meetings
- Daily check-in with manager recommended
- Response time: within 2 hours during work hours

## Security

- VPN required for all remote connections
- Confidential documents must not be printed at home
- Lock screen when away from computer
- Report any security incidents immediately

## Performance Expectations

- Same productivity standards as in-office work
- Regular performance reviews include remote work assessment
- Failure to meet standards may result in revocation of remote work privilege

## Contact

For remote work requests, contact your direct manager and HR at hr@company.com
