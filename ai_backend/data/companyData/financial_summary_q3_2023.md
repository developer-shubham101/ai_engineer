# Agni Holdings Inc. - Q3 2023 Financial Highlights (Confidential)

## Executive Summary
Q3 has been a quarter of stabilization and strategic investment. While revenue growth in the Pharma sector has flattened (-2%), the IT and Logistics sectors have outperformed expectations (+15%).

## Key Metrics
*   **Total Group Revenue:** $850 Million (Q3)
*   **EBITDA:** $120 Million
*   **Net Profit Margin:** 14.1%

## Subsidiary Performance
1.  **Saarthi Infotech:** Generated $310M. Strong growth in Cloud Migration services for FinTech clients.
2.  **Agni Pharma Innovations:** Generated $200M. R&D costs for "Project Surya" impacted short-term margins.
3.  **Praxis Global Logistics:** Generated $180M. The new "Green-Route" algorithm reduced fuel costs by 12%.
4.  **Vajra Solutions:** Generated $110M. Licensing revenue for AI models is up 40% YoY.
5.  **Nectar Digital:** Generated $50M.

## Forward-Looking Statements
CFO Eleanor Vance has approved a budget increase of $50M for Q4 to acquire a boutique cybersecurity firm in Israel (Code name: Target-Zion) to bolster Saarthi's security offerings.
