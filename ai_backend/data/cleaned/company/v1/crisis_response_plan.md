Agni Holdings - Crisis Management Protocol (CMP)
Severity Level: RED
Scenario: Cyber Ransomware Attack
If a "Ransomware" screen is detected on any Agni system:
Isolate: DO NOT turn off the machine. Disconnect the LAN cable and turn off Wi-Fi immediately.
Report: Call the Emergency IT Hotline (+91-22-6789-9999).
Communication:
Do not use Teams or Outlook (as they might be compromised).
Use the backup communication app "Signal" group "Agni-Crisis-Core".
Spokesperson: Only Maria Rodriguez (CLCO) is authorized to speak to the press/media.
Scenario: Chemical Spill (Agni Pharma Labs)
Evacuate: Trigger the blue alarm. Evacuate the floor immediately.
Contain: Only trained Hazmat personnel (Team Alpha) should attempt containment.
Medical: Transport affected personnel to St. Jude’s Hospital (our medical partner).
