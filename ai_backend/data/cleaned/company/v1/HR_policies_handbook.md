Agni Holdings Inc. - Global Employee Handbook (2024 Edition)
1. Attendance and Work Hours
Agni Holdings operates on a flexible work schedule to accommodate our global presence across Mumbai, New York, and other hubs.
*   Core Hours: Employees must be available between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM local time.
*   Standard Week: 40 hours per week, Monday through Friday.
*   Remote Work: All employees at Saarthi Infotech and Vajra Solutions are eligible for "Remote-First" work. Employees at Agni Pharma (Lab staff) and Praxis (Logistics on-site) must adhere to the "3-days-in-office" hybrid policy.
2. Leave Policy (Global Standard)
Privilege Leave (PL): 21 days annually (accrued monthly). Can be carried forward up to 45 days.
Casual/Sick Leave (CL/SL): 12 days annually. Cannot be carried forward.
Wellness Days: 4 "Unplug" days per year, mandated by CEO Aisha Sharma to prevent burnout. These must be taken once per quarter.
Parental Leave: 26 weeks for primary caregivers; 4 weeks for secondary caregivers.
3. Code of Conduct
Moonlighting: Strictly prohibited. Employees cannot hold secondary employment without written consent from HR and Legal.
Dress Code: Smart Casual. Formal attire is required only for client meetings.
Conflict of Interest: Any personal relationship with a vendor or competitor must be disclosed to the 'Ethics Committee' within 7 days.
4. Compensation & Benefits
Salary Disbursal: Last working day of the month.
Variable Pay: Performance bonuses are calculated based on the 'Agni Scorecard' distributed in April.
Insurance: Global health coverage is provided by 'SecureLife World,' covering the employee + spouse + 2 children.
