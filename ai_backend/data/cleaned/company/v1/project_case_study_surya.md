Internal Case Study: Project Surya
Client: MediGuard Pharmaceuticals (External)
Lead Agni Subsidiary: Agni Pharma Innovations (API)
Supporting Subsidiary: Vajra Solutions
Project Overview
Project Surya was a high-stakes initiative to accelerate the clinical trial phase for MediGuard's new hypertension drug, CardioFix-X. Traditional timelines estimated a 12-month trial phase.
The Solution
Data Synthesis: Vajra Solutions deployed its "Thunderbolt-AI" model to analyze 15 years of historical patient data from MediGuard.
Patient Screening: Agni Pharma used this data to identify ideal trial candidates with 98% accuracy, filtering out those likely to have adverse reactions based on genetic markers.
Monitoring: Real-time patient vitals were tracked using IoT wearables integrated by Saarthi Infotech.
Results
Time Saved: Trial phase reduced from 12 months to 7 months.
Cost Reduction: $4.5 Million USD saved in operational costs.
Outcome: CardioFix-X received regulatory approval 5 months ahead of schedule.
