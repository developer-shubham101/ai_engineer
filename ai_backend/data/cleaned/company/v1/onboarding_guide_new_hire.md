Welcome to the Tribe: New Hire Onboarding Guide
Day 1 Checklist
ID Badge: Collect your RFID access card from the Security Desk (Ground Floor). This card works for the cafeteria and gym.
IT Setup: Login to your laptop using the temporary credentials sent to your personal email.
HR Orientation: Attend the "Ignite" session at 11:00 AM (Conference Room B or Teams Link).
The "Agni Buddy" Program
Every new hire is assigned a "Buddy" from a different department. Your buddy is there to answer non-work questions (e.g., "Where is the best coffee?", "How do I claim expenses?").
*   Action: Check your inbox for an intro email from your Buddy today!
Key Tools You Will Use
Communication: Microsoft Teams
HR Portal: "Agni-People" (Use this for leave and tax documents).
Project Management: Jira (for IT/Eng), Asana (for Marketing), Trello (for Admin).
Cafeteria Rules
Breakfast: 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM (Free).
Lunch: 12:30 PM - 2:30 PM (Subsidized).
Coffee/Tea: Available 24/7 at pantry stations.
