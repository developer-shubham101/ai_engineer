Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Report 2023
Theme: "Knowledge is Fire"
The Agni Foundation
The Agni Foundation manages 2% of the group's net profit for social impact activities.
Key Initiatives Completed
Project Vidya (Education):
Built 15 computer labs in rural schools near Bengaluru.
Donated 500 refurbished laptops from Saarthi Infotech to underprivileged students.
Project Sanjeevani (Health):
Agni Pharma organized 50 free health camps in Maharashtra and Gujarat.
Distributed 10,000 free kits of generic medicines for diabetes management.
Tech for Good:
Vajra Solutions developed a free app for farmers to predict crop diseases using AI. The app, "Kisan-Drishti," has 50,000 active users.
Volunteer Hours
Agni employees contributed a record-breaking 12,500 volunteer hours in 2023. Top volunteering department: Nectar Digital.
