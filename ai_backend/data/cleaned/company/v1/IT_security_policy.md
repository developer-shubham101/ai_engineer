Group IT Security Protocol - Agni Holdings
Issued by: Dr. Vikram Singh, Group CTO
Effective Date: Jan 1, 2024
1. Password Policy
All passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
Must contain: Uppercase, Lowercase, Number, and Special Character.
Password rotation is mandatory every 90 days.
Note: The previous policy of 60 days has been extended to 90 days based on employee feedback.
2. Device Usage & BYOD
Authorized Devices: Only company-issued laptops (Dell Latitude for Business / MacBook Pro for Design & Dev) are allowed to access the internal network 'AgniNet'.
BYOD (Bring Your Own Device): Personal mobile devices may access Outlook and Teams only if 'Intune Company Portal' is installed and active.
3. Data Classification
Level 1 (Public): Marketing materials, Press releases.
Level 2 (Internal): Employee directories, Memos.
Level 3 (Confidential): Client code (Saarthi), Logistics routes (Praxis).
Level 4 (Restricted): Pharma formulas (API), Patient data, M&A strategies.
Constraint: Level 4 data cannot be stored on local drives or USB sticks. It must remain in the secure 'Vajra-Vault' cloud storage.
4. Incident Reporting
In the event of a suspected breach (phishing, lost laptop), employees must contact the SOC (Security Operations Center) immediately at soc-alert@agniholdings.com or dial extension 9999 internally.
