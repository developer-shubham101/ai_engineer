Agni Holdings & Subsidiaries: Brand Identity Guidelines (v4.0)
Managed by: Nectar Digital Marketing & Experience
Our Core Philosophy
Agni means "Fire." Our visual identity must convey warmth, energy, transformation, and enlightenment. We are not aggressive; we are illuminating.
Color Palette
Agni Red (Primary): Hex #D32F2F | CMYK (13, 97, 100, 4) - Used for the main logo and call-to-action buttons.
Ash Grey (Secondary): Hex #616161 - Used for body text and footer backgrounds.
Solar Orange (Accent): Hex #FF6F00 - Used for highlights and innovation-related graphics.
Clean White: Hex #FFFFFF - Agni embraces whitespace to symbolize clarity of thought.
Typography
Headings: Montserrat (Bold/Extra Bold). To be used for all titles.
Body Copy: Open Sans (Regular). Ensures readability across global scripts.
Code/Technical: Fira Code. For all IT and Developer documentation.
Logo Usage Rules
Clear Space: Always maintain a clear space equivalent to the height of the "A" in Agni around the logo.
Don'ts:
Never rotate the logo.
Never display the logo in green or blue (these are competitor colors).
Do not add shadow effects to the logo mark.
Voice & Tone
We are: Confident, Visionary, Empathetic, Precise.
We are not: Arrogant, Slang-heavy, Overly bureaucratic.
Example: Instead of saying "We fixed the bug," say "We optimized the system for better stability."
