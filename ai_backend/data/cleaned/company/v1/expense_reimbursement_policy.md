Global Travel & Expense Policy
Managed by: Finance Dept (CFO Office)
1. Air Travel
Leadership (VP & above): Business Class allowed for flights longer than 6 hours.
General Staff: Economy Class for all domestic flights. Premium Economy allowed for international flights over 8 hours.
2. Accommodation
Tier 1 Cities (NY, London, Tokyo): Cap of $250 USD per night.
Tier 2 Cities: Cap of $150 USD per night.
Preferred Partners: Marriott and Hilton groups (Use corporate code: AGNI2024).
3. Per Diem (Food & Incidentals)
Employees traveling on business are entitled to a flat per diem of $60 USD (Domestic) or $100 USD (International) per day. Receipts are not required for per diem claims.
4. Client Entertainment
Cap of $150 USD per person for client dinners.
Alcohol is reimbursable only if a client is present and it is culturally appropriate.
Approval: Any expense over $500 requires prior email approval from the Department Head.
