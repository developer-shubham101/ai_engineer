# Remote Work Policy

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2025  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Department:** HR  
**Document Type:** Policy  
**Changes:** Expanded to 3 days per week remote work

## Overview

This policy establishes updated guidelines for remote work arrangements within the company, responding to employee feedback and industry trends.

## Eligibility

Remote work is available to employees who:
- Have completed 3 months of employment (reduced from 6 months)
- Have received manager approval
- Have suitable home office setup
- Maintain consistent performance standards

## Remote Work Schedule

- Employees may work remotely **up to 3 days per week** (increased from 1 day)
- Remote workdays should be coordinated with team but don't require advance approval for each instance
- Core hours (10 AM - 4 PM) must be maintained
- Mondays recommended for in-office collaboration
- Team meetings can be conducted remotely via video

## Equipment and Expenses

- Company provides laptop and necessary software
- **NEW:** Employees receive $50/month internet stipend
- **NEW:** One-time $300 home office setup allowance
- Office supplies available for pickup from office or shipped to home
- Ergonomic equipment available upon request with manager approval

## Communication Requirements

- Must be available on company chat during work hours
- Video conferencing required for all team meetings
- Daily check-in with manager (async updates acceptable)
- Response time: within 1 hour during core hours (improved from 2 hours)

## Security

- VPN required for all remote connections
- Confidential documents must not be printed at home
- Lock screen when away from computer
- Two-factor authentication mandatory
- Report any security incidents immediately

## Performance Expectations

- Same productivity standards as in-office work
- Regular performance reviews include remote work assessment
- Quarterly remote work surveys to assess effectiveness
- Policy may be adjusted based on team and individual performance

## Hybrid Work Best Practices

- Schedule in-office days to overlap with team members
- Use calendar to indicate remote vs. office days
- Keep home workspace professional for video calls
- Maintain work-life boundaries

## Contact

For remote work requests, contact your direct manager and HR at hr@company.com
