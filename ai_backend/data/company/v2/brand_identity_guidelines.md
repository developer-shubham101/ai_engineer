# Saarthi Infotech & Agni Holdings: Brand Identity Guidelines (v5.0)
**Managed by:** Nectar Digital Marketing & Experience

## Our Core Philosophy
Saarthi means "Guide" and Agni means "Fire." Our visual identity must convey guidance, transformation, intelligence, and innovation. We are the trusted guide in the AI-powered enterprise landscape.

## Color Palette
* **Saarthi Blue (Primary):** Hex `#1976D2` | CMYK (85, 45, 0, 12) - Used for the main logo and AI system interfaces.
* **Agni Red (Secondary):** Hex `#D32F2F` | CMYK (13, 97, 100, 4) - Used for call-to-action buttons and alerts.
* **Neural Grey (Accent):** Hex `#424242` - Used for body text and system backgrounds.
* **Clean White:** Hex `#FFFFFF` - Saarthi embraces whitespace to symbolize clarity of AI insights.

## Typography
* **Headings:** *Inter* (Bold/Extra Bold). Modern, clean font for AI system interfaces.
* **Body Copy:** *Source Sans Pro* (Regular). Ensures readability across global scripts and technical documentation.
* **Code/Technical:** *JetBrains Mono*. For all AI, ML, and Developer documentation.

## Logo Usage Rules
1. **Clear Space:** Always maintain a clear space equivalent to the height of the "S" in Saarthi around the logo.
2. **Don'ts:**
   * Never rotate the logo.
   * Never display the logo in green (competitor color).
   * Do not add shadow effects to the logo mark.
   * Never use the logo on busy AI-generated backgrounds.

## Voice & Tone for AI Systems
* **We are:** Intelligent, Trustworthy, Precise, Innovative.
* **We are not:** Robotic, Overly technical, Condescending.
* *Example:* Instead of saying "Query processed," say "I've found relevant information for you."

## AI System Branding
* All RAG responses should maintain consistent tone
* Multi-provider system should feel unified
* Local and cloud models should have same brand experience
