# Annual Leave Policy

**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Department:** HR  
**Document Type:** Policy  
**Changes:** Increased to 25 days, added unlimited sick leave, flexible scheduling

## Overview

This document outlines the company's enhanced annual leave policy for all full-time employees, effective March 1, 2025. This update reflects our commitment to employee wellbeing and work-life balance.

## Annual Leave Entitlement

All full-time employees are entitled to **25 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.

### What Changed
- **v1.0:** 15 days annual leave
- **v2.0:** 20 days annual leave  
- **v3.0:** 25 days annual leave (Current)
- **Effective:** March 1, 2025

### Eligibility
- Employees become eligible for annual leave **immediately upon hire** (no waiting period)
- Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2.08 days per month
- Unused leave can be carried forward up to **10 days** to the next year (increased from 7 days)
- **NEW:** No cap on medical/sick leave (unlimited, with documentation after 3 consecutive days)

## Leave Application Process

### Standard Leave
1. Submit leave request at least 1 week in advance (reduced from 2 weeks)
2. Manager approval required (auto-approved if within team coverage limits)
3. HR notification automatic via employee portal
4. Last-minute leave (< 48 hours notice) allowed up to 3 times per year

### Emergency Leave
- Same-day notification acceptable
- Documentation may be requested after the fact
- No penalty for genuine emergencies

## Public Holidays

In addition to annual leave, employees receive:
- 10 public holidays per year
- Public holidays falling on weekends compensated with a floating day off
- **NEW:** "Floating Wellness Days" - 2 additional days per year to be taken at employee's discretion

## Special Circumstances

### Medical Leave
- **UNLIMITED sick leave** with appropriate documentation
- Doctor's note required after 3 consecutive sick days
- Chronic condition accommodations available

### Family Leave
- Maternity leave: 16 weeks paid
- Paternity leave: 8 weeks paid
- Adoption leave: 12 weeks paid
- Parental leave: 4 weeks paid (for childcare emergencies)

### Other Leave
- Bereavement leave: 5 days paid for immediate family (increased from 3 days)
- Jury duty: Fully paid, not deducted from annual leave
- Volunteer day: 2 paid days per year for approved volunteer activities

## Year-End Carryover

- Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried to next year
- Leave beyond 10 days will be forfeited
- Option to cash out up to 7 unused days at year-end (increased from 5)
- **NEW:** "Leave donation program" - donate unused days to colleagues in need

## Sabbatical Leave

- **NEW:** Employees with 5+ years tenure eligible for 4-week paid sabbatical
- Sabbatical available once every 5 years
- Must be taken in 2-4 week blocks
- Requires 3 months advance planning

## Mental Health Days

- **NEW:** Employees can designate up to 5 annual leave days as "mental health days"
- No questions asked, no documentation required
- Can be taken with 24 hours notice

## Contact

For questions regarding this policy, contact HR at hr@company.com or use the employee portal's leave calculator.
