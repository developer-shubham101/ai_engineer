# Internal Memo: From the Desk of the CEO

**To:** All Agni Holdings Employees
**From:** Aisha Sharma, Group CEO
**Date:** October 15, 2023
**Subject:** Embracing the "Agni 2.0" Sustainable Future

Team,

As we approach the end of another transformative year, I want to reiterate our commitment to the "Agni 2.0" vision. It is no longer enough to be profitable; we must be sustainable.

I am proud to announce that Agni Holdings has pledged to become **Carbon Neutral by 2030**.

To achieve this:
1.  **Praxis Global** will transition 50% of its delivery fleet to electric vehicles by 2025.
2.  **Saarthi Infotech** is migrating all data centers to Green Energy providers.
3.  **Agni Pharma** is reducing plastic waste in packaging by 30% next year.

This is not just a policy; it is our promise to the next generation. I expect every department head to present a sustainability roadmap for their unit by November 30th.

Let us burn bright, but burn clean.

Warm regards,
Aisha Sharma
Group CEO
