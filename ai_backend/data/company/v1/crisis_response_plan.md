# Agni Holdings - Crisis Management Protocol (CMP)
**Severity Level: RED**

## Scenario: Cyber Ransomware Attack
If a "Ransomware" screen is detected on any Agni system:

1.  **Isolate:** DO NOT turn off the machine. Disconnect the LAN cable and turn off Wi-Fi immediately.
2.  **Report:** Call the Emergency IT Hotline (+91-22-6789-9999).
3.  **Communication:**
    *   Do not use Teams or Outlook (as they might be compromised).
    *   Use the backup communication app "Signal" group "Agni-Crisis-Core".
    *   **Spokesperson:** Only Maria Rodriguez (CLCO) is authorized to speak to the press/media.

## Scenario: Chemical Spill (Agni Pharma Labs)
1.  **Evacuate:** Trigger the blue alarm. Evacuate the floor immediately.
2.  **Contain:** Only trained Hazmat personnel (Team Alpha) should attempt containment.
3.  **Medical:** Transport affected personnel to *St. Jude’s Hospital* (our medical partner).
