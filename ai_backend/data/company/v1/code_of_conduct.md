# Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Department:** HR  
**Document Type:** Policy

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct outlines expected behaviors and ethical standards for all employees.

## Core Values

1. **Integrity** - Act honestly and ethically in all business dealings
2. **Respect** - Treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with dignity
3. **Excellence** - Strive for quality in all work
4. **Accountability** - Take responsibility for actions and decisions

## Professional Conduct

### Workplace Behavior
- Maintain professional demeanor at all times
- Dress appropriately for your role and client interactions
- Use professional language in all communications
- Respect working hours and deadlines

### Harassment and Discrimination
- Zero tolerance for harassment of any kind
- No discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, or disability
- Report violations immediately to HR
- Retaliation against reporters is prohibited

## Confidentiality

- Protect company proprietary information
- Do not share client data outside authorized channels
- Sign confidentiality agreements as required
- Return all confidential materials upon termination

## Conflict of Interest

- Disclose any potential conflicts to management
- Do not accept gifts exceeding $50 from clients or vendors
- Outside employment must not interfere with company duties
- Family relationships in reporting structure must be disclosed

## Use of Company Resources

- Company equipment for business purposes only
- Limited personal use of email acceptable
- No downloading unauthorized software
- Social media use must not harm company reputation

## Reporting Violations

- Report violations to your manager or HR
- Anonymous hotline available: 1-800-XXX-XXXX
- All reports investigated confidentially
- No retaliation for good faith reports

## Consequences

Violations may result in:
- Verbal or written warning
- Suspension
- Termination of employment
- Legal action if warranted

## Acknowledgment

All employees must sign acknowledgment of this Code of Conduct upon hire and annually thereafter.
