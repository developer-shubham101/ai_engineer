# Project Proposal: "Green Sky"
**Department:** Praxis Global Logistics Tech
**Status:** Pending Board Approval
**Budget Request:** $15 Million USD

## Objective
To develop a proprietary fleet of autonomous cargo drones for "Last Mile" delivery of critical medicines in remote Himalayan regions.

## The Problem
Currently, delivering vaccines to remote villages in Nepal and Northern India takes 4-5 days via road, leading to spoilage.

## The Proposed Solution
*   **Hardware:** Custom-built hexacopter drones (Payload: 5kg, Range: 50km).
*   **Software:** Vajra Solutions' navigation AI to handle high-altitude wind currents.
*   **Partnership:** Collaborate with local governments for airspace clearance.

## Timeline
*   **Phase 1 (Q1 2024):** Prototype testing in the Alps.
*   **Phase 2 (Q3 2024):** Pilot program in Ladakh, India.
*   **Phase 3 (2025):** Full commercial deployment.

## ROI Analysis
While the initial cost is high, "Green Sky" opens up a monopoly market in high-altitude logistics, estimated to be worth $50M annually by 2026.
