# Master Service Agreement (MSA) - Logistics Services
**Contract ID:** PRAX-LOG-2024-09
**Date:** January 10, 2024

**Between:**
1.  **Praxis Global Logistics Tech** (The "Buyer")
    *Address:* Agni Tower, Mumbai.
2.  **Poseidon Ocean Liners Ltd.** (The "Provider")
    *Address:* 88 Maritime Way, Singapore.

## 1. Scope of Services
Poseidon Ocean Liners shall provide cold-chain shipping containers for the transport of pharmaceutical raw materials between Mumbai (IN) and Hamburg (DE).

## 2. Rate Structure
*   **Standard 20ft Container:** $1,200 USD per unit.
*   **Reefer (Refrigerated) Unit:** $2,800 USD per unit.
*   **Fuel Surcharge (BAF):** Capped at 5% of base freight.

## 3. Service Level Agreement (SLA)
*   **Transit Time:** Port-to-Port transit must not exceed 22 days.
*   **Temperature Integrity:** Reefer units must maintain a temperature between 2°C and 8°C at all times.

## 4. Penalties
*   **Late Delivery:** A penalty of $500 per day applies for delays exceeding the 22-day limit.
*   **Temperature Excursion:** If data logs show temperature deviation outside the 2°C-8°C range for more than 2 hours, the Buyer (Praxis) reserves the right to claim 100% of the cargo value ($50,000 average per container).

## 5. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement with 60 days written notice.
